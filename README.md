## Overview

LuaSwap is an open-source, community-governed exchange protocol that prioritizes transparency, fair reward distribution, and accessible participation. Every line of code is public, every fee is traceable on-chain, and 100% of protocol revenue flows to community participants — zero team or treasury cut.

Aggregated liquidity routing, concentrated liquidity CLMM pools, cross-chain bridging across 12+ networks, multi-tier staking, and governance-driven fee distribution — all in one non-custodial protocol.

---

## Key Features

### ⚡ Sub-400ms Execution
Parallel transaction processing delivers trade finality in under 400ms with MEV protection.

### 🔀 Smart Order Routing
Queries all liquidity sources and routes orders through the optimal combination for best price.

### 🔓 Radical Transparency Model
Zero team/treasury fee cut — 100% of all protocol revenue goes directly to LPs and stakers. Every fee collection and distribution is verifiable on-chain in real-time via the analytics dashboard. All code open-source. All governance on-chain. Full community control.

### 💧 Concentrated Liquidity (CLMM)
Custom price range LP positions, up to 10× capital efficiency. Top pools: 24.8% APR.

### 🌉 Cross-Chain Bridge
12+ networks, 10–20 min, guardian-secured. ETH, SOL, USDC, USDT, wBTC, BNB, AVAX.

### 🏆 Real Yield
100% of fees to stakers and LPs. Zero inflationary emissions.
